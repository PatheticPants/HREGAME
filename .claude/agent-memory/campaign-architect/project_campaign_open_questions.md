Open, not decided. Do not present any of these as settled features.

1. **REFER is currently free.** It is the office's procedure, it is always defensible on an
   authority split, and it costs the player nothing in candle, money or future slots. Until
   it costs something, the third ring is a safety valve rather than a decision.
2. **What surface does favour act through?** Candidate answers proposed but not accepted:
   which reference pages the office lets you keep; which morning letters arrive; whether the
   doorkeeper annotates dockets. Must stay legible as *stable interests*, per the campaign law
   that a faction cannot love an act in one case and hate it in another.
3. **What is the money sink, and what may money never buy?** Agreed-in-principle only:
   money may buy candle (throughput), wax quality (craft reliability), and updated books
   (access). It may never buy correctness.
4. **How is the election caused rather than revealed?** Leading candidate is a *derived*
   holdings ledger (which authority this office has put each `subject_id` into), rendered as
   a generated book the way `RegisterBook.build()` already generates the Register. Not built.
5. **Kammerknechtschaft / imperial protection charters.** Historically rich and ethically
   delicate. If used at all, keep the mechanic documentary (who currently holds a pledged
   regalian right) rather than about the protected community.

**Why:** these came out of the 2026-07-28 gameplay-loop review of the shipped two-day slice.

**How to apply:** when proposing, say explicitly which of these a proposal answers, and do not
silently assume an earlier answer was accepted. See [[project-campaign-state]].
