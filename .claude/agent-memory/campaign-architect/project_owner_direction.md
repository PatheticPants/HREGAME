The owner's verbatim direction (given 2026-07-28) for the campaign phase:

> "really focus on the gameplay loop and events. This should be a very interesting unique and
> fun to play game. Keep a relative 'historical accurate' base. Make sure there is a hook and a
> continuous engaging story/gameplay. Maybe we add in some twists later, we will pin this
> though, we can maybe set up something that will lead to twists later for right now. Don't
> forget to have some funny but keep it dry and subtle."

Four standing requirements that follow from it:
1. A **hook** — a reason to start, and a distinct reason to come back after matter one.
2. **Continuous** engagement across days, not a demo that repeats.
3. Twists are **PINNED**: plant seeds now, fire nothing. Every seed must be satisfying and
   inert on its own terms if it is never pulled.
4. Humour is **dry, subtle, rationed** — see [[campaign-invariants]] for the exact budget and
   the forbidden slots.

**Why:** the two-day slice proves the machinery works but does not yet prove anyone would play
a third day. The owner is asking for the campaign shape, not more cases.

**How to apply:** reject any proposal that resolves R.V., the predecessor, or the election.
Judge proposals on whether the fourth petitioner is more compelling because of the first three.
See [[project-campaign-state]] and [[campaign-open-questions]].
