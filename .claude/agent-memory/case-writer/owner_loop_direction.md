Standing direction from the owner, given verbatim on 2026-07-28 and pinned:
focus on the **gameplay loop and events**; the game must have a **hook** and
**continuous engaging story/gameplay**; keep a **relatively historically accurate
base**; **set up things that will lead to twists later but do not fire them yet**
("we will pin this though"); and keep humour **funny but dry and subtle**.

**Why:** the build is mechanically strong (verification, candle, Register,
precedent) and structurally thin — two days, eight cases, one repeated beat shape.
The owner is not asking for more verification types; they are asking for reasons
to come back after matter one. They explicitly deferred twists rather than
cancelling them, so twist material should be *planted as inert content now* and
left unresolved.

**How to apply:**
- Judge any content proposal first on whether it changes what the *next* day feels
  like, not on whether it adds a new defect to find.
- Anything twist-shaped must read as ordinary office life if it is never
  explained. R.V. (the predecessor, ~23 marginalia + the seeded Register + the
  memorandum "TO THE THIRD HAND") is the existing example of this done right.
- Humour is hard-rationed: 6-8 dry beats per working day, never two in one matter,
  flat declarative, joke in a subordinate clause, and it must carry information.
  At least three beats per day must live inside a book the player has to open.
  There is a written forbidden list in `docs/CONTINUITY.md` — read it before
  adding a line.
- "Invent polities and people, not procedures." Chancery practice, diplomatic,
  sealing, regnal dating, partible inheritance, ministeriales and obit rolls
  should behave like the real things. Polities are capped at 6-9; adding one
  requires naming what it displaces.
